What is OKLCH? The Modern CSS Color Space
OKLCH is a modern cylindrical representation of the Oklab color space designed specifically for human vision. It fixes the mathematical flaws of RGB and HSL by ensuring that identical numerical lightness values look equally bright across all hues.
